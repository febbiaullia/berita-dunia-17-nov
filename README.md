# The World Today — 17 November 2026

One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026, for the
make.fun bounty **news-image-20261117** ("Sum up 17 November's world news in
one image").

## The stories in the picture

- **Ukraine smashes the Crimea Bridge** — Kyiv's second strike in two days
  drops the road arch into the Kerch Strait, as Russia pounds bridges in Kyiv.
- **Iran drones strike a US radar site in Bahrain** — Tehran's third attack in
  days on US targets in the Gulf; Bahrain says it was intercepted.
- **Xi, Trump and Putin to meet in Shenzhen** — China confirms a first
  three-way summit on Thursday at APEC.
- **Tankers hit near the Strait of Hormuz** — mines and a projectile strike
  ships as Gulf tensions rise.
- **Congo Ebola tops 11,000 cases** — WHO says the outbreak has passed 11,000
  cases, with more than 5,400 deaths.
- **Also today:** Nvidia revenue doubles to $115.8bn; Dhoni retires from IPL;
  Britain sanctions settlement firms; Canada-China agree tariff cuts.

## How it runs

The poster is a single 1024x1024 PNG held in this repository. The program
copies it to the output directory as `entry.png`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
